What we make

Crown and bridge, implants, and appliances.

Restorations are designed digitally and milled on precision 5-axis equipment, then finished and checked by hand before they leave the lab.

  • Zirconia crowns and bridges, monolithic and layered
  • e.max (lithium disilicate) crowns
  • PFM, PFZ and full cast gold crowns
  • Implant restorations: custom titanium abutments, screw-retained crowns and verification jigs
  • Night guards and occlusal splints, 3D printed
  • Surgical guides
  • Diagnostic wax-ups with printed models

Questions

Common questions from Phoenix offices.

Do you accept iTero scans?

Yes. We accept iTero, 3Shape TRIOS, Medit, Carestream and open STL files, as well as traditional impressions.

How does pickup work in Phoenix?

We offer free same-day local pickup across the Valley, including Phoenix, Scottsdale, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Peoria and Paradise Valley. Offices outside pickup range get free two-way shipping with prepaid labels.

What is your standard turnaround?

Xpress, our standard, is 48 hours. Next-day service is available on request, and Scheduled runs seven business days at a preferred rate.

Can I visit the lab?

Yes. The lab is at 3030 N Central Ave, Suite 500, Phoenix, open Monday through Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM, with Saturday visits by appointment.

Are there minimums or contracts?

No. There are no minimums, no monthly fees and no contracts. Each case is charged when it ships.
