Privacy policy

Your information, handled simply.

Effective November 18, 2026

SmileXpress Dental Studio, a Sonoran Dental Technologies company, operates smilexpress.io. This page explains what information the website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

The website collects information only when you choose to send it to us through a form, such as a fee schedule request. That includes:

  • Your practice name
  • Your email address
  • Your name, if you provide it
  • The name of a colleague who referred you, if you provide it

We also receive anything you choose to include when you call or email us.

What we do not collect

  • No cookies or tracking. The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising pixels, or other tracking tools.
  • No patient information. Please do not send patient health information through the website or its forms. Cases and patient details belong in the Doctor Portal.
  • No payment details. We never collect card or bank details on the website.

How we use your information

  • To send the fee schedule or other information you requested
  • To reply to your questions and help you open an account
  • To credit referrals under our referral program

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Who helps us handle it

We rely on a small number of service providers, who handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • FormSubmit delivers website form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Workspace hosts our business email.
  • Netlify hosts the website and keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

When you open an account, the Doctor Portal is provided by SimpleLabOS, and payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe stores your card or bank details; we never see the full numbers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions and related email for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our business and legal recordkeeping needs, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you have sent us at any time. Email info@smilexpress.io and we will respond promptly.

Children

This website is intended for dental professionals and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles information, for example by adding analytics, we will update this page and the effective date above.
