The background

DSO-scale production, built for independent practices.
Zach TandyFounder, SmileXpress Dental Studio

For twelve years I built and ran the internal lab for a multi-state dental group, scaling it as the organization grew from 8 practices to 27 across four states.

Every unit that lab produced went to a chair that had to stay on schedule, in front of a doctor whose name was on the result. No price advantage ever fixed a late case. Neither did an apology.

SmileXpress runs on the same production systems, quality checks, and scheduling discipline, now available to practices without an in-house lab.

How it works

From scan to seat in four steps.

STEP 01

Send the case

Digital scan from iTero, 3Shape TRIOS, Medit, or Carestream. Open STL files and traditional impressions are equally welcome.

STEP 02

Submit the lab slip

Build it in the Doctor Portal with photos and shade images. It writes directly into production, so the technician reads exactly what you wrote.

STEP 03

We design, mill and verify

Designed to your prescription, precision milled or printed, and checked against the model before it leaves.

STEP 04

Delivered ready to seat

Arrives on the committed date, verified, with free two-way shipping on prepaid labels.
